---
id: weekly-review
name: Weekly review
description: Use when the user asks to review their week or plan the next one, or when a weekly job runs.
---
Needs: nothing beyond your memories. A calendar integration makes step 3 better.

1. Ask what got done this week, or list what you know got done from your memories and conversations, and ask them to fill the gaps.
2. Ask what didn't get done, and for each one whether to carry it over, drop it, or hand it to someone else.
3. Look at next week: their calendar if you have it, plus follow-ups and anything they mentioned is coming up.
4. Ask for the one or two things that matter most next week.
5. Sum it up in a short list: done, carried over, dropped, next week's priorities.
6. Offer to `remember` the priorities and to `set_followup` on any of them mid-week.

Done when: the user has agreed to the summary, and anything they wanted remembered is saved.

Watch out:
- This is a conversation, not a report. Ask one question at a time and wait for the answer.
- Don't judge what didn't get done. Ask what to do with it.
