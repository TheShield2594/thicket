name: Weekly meal plan
description: Use when the household asks for a week of meals, or when the Sunday meal plan routine runs.
---
Needs: family memory for diets, allergies and favourites. The household calendar (if a sprig with it can tell you) makes step 2 better.

1. Check what you know: who's eating, diets, allergies, dislikes, and meals that went down well. Ask only for what's missing.
2. Ask which nights are busy or out, and what's already in the fridge or cupboard that needs using.
3. Plan five to seven dinners: quick ones on busy nights, at least one leftovers night, nothing that breaks an allergy or diet.
4. Send the plan as a short list, one line per day, and nothing else around it.
5. Offer the shopping list (the `shopping-list` skill), and offer to `remember` any new favourite or dislike they mention.

Done when: the household has a plan they're happy with, and the shopping list if they wanted it.

Watch out:
- Allergies are never "probably fine". If you aren't sure an ingredient is safe, leave it out and say so.
- Don't repeat a dinner from the last two weeks unless they ask for it.
- No nutrition lectures. Health only comes up if they asked for it.
- In a routine run: if someone already planned this week in chat, call `nothing_to_report`.
