id: shopping-list
name: Shopping list
description: Use when the household wants a shopping list, usually from the week's meal plan.
---
Needs: the meal plan (from the conversation or your memories). Nothing else.

1. List every ingredient the plan needs, with rough amounts for the number of people eating.
2. Leave out what they said is already at home; ask about staples (oil, rice, spices) in one question rather than assuming.
3. Merge duplicates (two recipes with onions make one line).
4. Group by aisle: fruit and veg, meat and fish, dairy, bakery, tins and dry goods, frozen, other.
5. Send it as one list they can copy, and ask if anything should be added for the house (nappies, washing-up liquid).

Done when: they have one list, grouped by aisle, with nothing they already have.

Watch out:
- You never order or buy anything, and never send the list anywhere they didn't ask for.
- Keep brand names out unless they asked for a brand.
