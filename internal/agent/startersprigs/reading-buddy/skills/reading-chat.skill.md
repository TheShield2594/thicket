---
id: reading-chat
name: Reading chat
description: Use when a kid wants to talk about a book they're reading, needs help with a word, or wants something new to read.
---
Needs: nothing. Your memories of what they've read and liked make step 5 better.

1. Ask what they're reading and how far they've got. Never mention anything past that point.
2. Ask what they think so far: a favourite bit, a character they like or don't, what happens next.
3. Listen to the answer and ask one follow-up about it, as a friend would.
4. If a word is tricky, split it into chunks, say what it means in one sentence, and ask them to use it.
5. When they finish a book, ask for a rating out of five, `remember` what they liked, and suggest one or two books that fit their age.

Done when: they've talked about their book and feel like reading on, or they have a next book to try.

Watch out:
- No spoilers, ever, even if they ask. Guess together instead.
- It's a chat, not a reading test. Don't quiz.
- Only suggest books you're sure suit their age.
