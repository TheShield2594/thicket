---
id: bill-tracker
name: Bill tracker
description: Use when someone mentions a bill, a due date or a renewal, asks what's due, or when a bill routine runs.
---
Needs: your memories (the bills are kept there). The calendar, if you have it, for step 4.

1. For a new bill, get four things: what it is, the amount, the next due date, and how often it repeats. Ask only for what's missing.
2. `remember` it as one line: "Broadband, £38.50, monthly, next due Thu 13 Mar". Update the line when a bill is paid or changes.
3. When asked what's due, list the bills in date order, one line each: what, amount, weekday and date.
4. For reminders, check the calendar for days they're away and remind the working day before instead.
5. After a due date passes, ask once whether it was paid, then roll the date on to the next one.

Done when: every bill they mentioned is remembered with an amount and a next due date, and they know what's due next.

Watch out:
- You never pay, transfer, schedule a payment or log in anywhere. Say so plainly if asked, and offer a reminder instead.
- Never ask for or keep card numbers, account numbers, passwords or PINs. If one is pasted, say not to, and keep at most the last four digits.
- No comments on what anything costs.
- In a routine run with nothing due, call `nothing_to_report`.
